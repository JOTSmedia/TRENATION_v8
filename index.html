Bio

From church choir to stadium stages.

Tré Nation grew up in San Antonio, one of six kids, singing Disney ballads at four and gospel in church by seven. He recorded gospel music from 2007 to 2013 and sang with the San Antonio band {Attica} before moving to Los Angeles at 25, where an agency sent him to sing with bands from San Diego to Beijing.

Then guitarist Johnny Baab spotted him on an Instagram story and asked him to audition. As lead singer of the Pittsburgh blues-rock band Ghost Hounds, signed to Gibson Records, Tré toured with ZZ Top, Bob Seger and The Rolling Stones, opening for the Stones in Washington, D.C. in 2019 and across Europe in 2022.

Now based in New York, Tré makes alt-pop with a soul singer's falsetto and a songwriter's honesty. His album MAGICK arrived October 16, 2025, after singles like “I'm In Love With A Man,” “Time,” “Lightspeed” and “Luv-Luv.” Off the mic he hosts Good Day with Tré and shares guided meditations with his fans.
